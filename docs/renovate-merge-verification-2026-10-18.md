## Bounded recovery and limits

The earlier hourly scan and bot-comment deletion were removed. There is no scheduled retry workflow or new credential. After an initially authorized Renovate merge fails, including CI failure or the internal wait timeout, the existing notifier may request the standard `rebase` label once per PR lifetime. It first records a durable Server App marker, and revalidates current control, exact accepted head, own ready PR, comment timeline, signatures, every effective bot committer and sensitive-path policy. Human-modified branches and signature, authorization or policy failures do not qualify. A control change requires a fresh request under the new runtime or manual Renovate retry.

Scratch verified the actual retry marker/label API behavior and exhaustion. No failure was deliberately injected into the hosted Renovate PR, so hosted failure-to-rebase scheduling is not separately certified by the successful merge above. Abrupt cancellation or runner loss can prevent the inline notifier from running. A crash after the marker can consume the allowance before label attachment. Further retry is manual, and Renovate's own scheduling determines when a requested rebase is processed.
